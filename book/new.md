### Chapter 5: Common Questions
**Core Message**: TGI answers questions with data, not emotions

- **Should I Buy Dips or Winners?**
  - Both questions miss the point
  - Real question: What's best for my portfolio?
  - TGI answer: Buy top-ranked stocks, period
  - If dip = top stock, buy the dip
  - If winner = top stock, buy more
  - Rankings cut through noise

- **Are Monthly Dividends or Specific Payout Months Worth It?**
  - Opportunity cost is too high
  - Monthly payers often lag in growth
  - My experience: Ignored monthly stocks to focus on growth
  - Income dip was painful but necessary
  - Once dividends = expenses, THEN optimize timing
  - Until then: Maximize growth

- **Should I Buy Overvalued Stocks at All-Time Highs?**
  - I invest for 10+ years, not 10 days
  - Question: Will future value exceed today's price?
  - If tops Buy List, I buy
  - Avoid only "outrageously overpriced" unless 2-3 year projections justify
  - 2025 policy dips tested this—top stocks held firm
  - Don't time the market; use rankings

- **Why Set Stocks to DRIP?**
  - Einstein: "Compound interest is the eighth wonder"
  - Plant fruit trees, build an orchard metaphor
  - Every stock I own = DRIP until retirement
  - 2023: Switched to "All In" DRIP
  - Previous toggle strategy was too complex
  - If not worth reinvesting, not worth owning
  - Keep DRIP on until you need cash for expenses

- **How to Handle a Large Capital Infusion?**
  - Inheritance, bonus, lottery win
  - TGI guides allocation with watchlist + diversity rules
  
  - **Up to $100K**:
    - High-growth ETFs (IUSG, SPYG) up to 10% in "Diversified" sector
    - Or top-ranked TGI stocks within diversity limits
  
  - **Near Retirement**:
    - Income ETFs (SPYD, SCHD)
    - Or HII-ranked stocks (Chapter 7)
  
  - **Millions (Lottery)**:
    - Invest it ALL in S&P 500
    - DON'T spend it (90% of winners go broke in 2-3 years)
    - Growth + dividends secure family forever
  
  - **Key Principle**: Don't let cash sit losing value to inflation

- **How to Invest Against Inflation?**
  - Cash loses 7-20% purchasing power yearly (since 1913)
  - Average inflation: 3.3% (not Fed's 2% target)
  - TGI counters by investing immediately in revenue-generating assets
  
  - **DCA vs. Lump Sum**:
    - DCA: For paychecks (slow addition of capital)
    - Lump sum: For sudden influx—invest immediately
  
  - **The Floor**: S&P 500 (7.46% average over 100 years)
  - Anything less (like bonds) = losing to inflation
  - If choice falters, sell and adjust
  - Don't let cash sit

- **Why Avoid Bonds?**
  - **Inflation is a Beast** (restore this powerful framing)
  - US Dollar loses 3.3% purchasing power yearly (1914-2024)
  - Bonds tied to fiat can't keep up
  - Traditional advice: 40% bonds for preservation
  - **I disagree strongly**: Bonds are traps
  - My $98K portfolio thrives on TGI stocks
  - NVIDIA: 1335% price growth over 5 years
  - S&P 500 is my floor
  - Growth, not safety, beats inflation
